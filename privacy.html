CoinVerse Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

1. Information Collected

CoinVerse may store Discord IDs, server IDs, usernames, economy data, inventories, and settings required for the bot to function.

2. Purpose

Collected information is used only to provide bot features and improve the service.

3. Data Sharing

CoinVerse does not sell or share personal data with third parties.

4. Data Removal

Server owners may request removal of their server's stored data by contacting the CoinVerse development team.

5. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time.
